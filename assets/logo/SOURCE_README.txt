8K by AK — 3D-print files
=======================

Units are millimeters. The logo is centered on X/Y and sits on Z=0 (flat on the build plate).

The thin coral divider in the original mark is too fine to print, so in these files it is a 1.6 mm bar at the 80 mm logo width. Scale the whole file uniformly if you need a different size. At much smaller than about 50 mm wide, the “by AK” text will get hard to print; use the 8K-only files for small branding on a model.

SVG (best for Fusion, Blender, Tinkercad, Illustrator — import and extrude yourself):
- 8K-by-AK-logo.svg     Full stacked lockup, 80 mm wide
- 8K-only.svg           Just the 8K wordmark, 80 mm wide (scale down as needed)

STL (ready to import or slice):
- 8K-by-AK-relief-80mm.stl   Raised logo only, 80 mm wide, 1.8 mm thick — union this onto a model
- 8K-by-AK-plaque-80mm.stl   Same logo on a 3 mm rounded plate — print as a standalone sign
- 8K-only-relief-50mm.stl    Just 8K, 50 mm wide, 1.8 mm thick — better for smaller models

To sink the logo into a surface instead of raising it, import the relief STL and use a boolean difference.

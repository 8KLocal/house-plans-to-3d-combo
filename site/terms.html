Terms

These terms cover custom architectural models from 8K Local Solutions, Brooklyn, NY, sold as 8K by AK. Last updated November 11, 2026.

Custom work, printed when you order

Every model is made from the plans you send. We print houses, buildings, and similar structures: homes, multi-family buildings, commercial buildings, additions, and developments. We do not sell stock models. We print on demand.

From your plan to printing

You place an order and upload your plan. We build the 3D model from that plan. It goes straight to the printer.

Prices

Package prices are $99, $149, and $199. Nothing is over $200. UPS shipping is added at cost. Pickup in Brooklyn is free. There is no minimum order, and quantity is not limited.

Printed models can vary slightly

A printed model can differ slightly from the drawing. That is part of printing a custom piece.

Shipping, pickup, and damage

We ship by UPS anywhere in the US with a tracking number, or you can pick up in Brooklyn at no charge. Estimated delivery: 5 to 10 business days. If a shipment arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours and include photos of the damage.

Refunds

We do not give refunds once printing has started, except when the problem is a defect in our work.

What we can refuse

We can refuse copyrighted or trademarked characters, weapons or weapon parts, and anything illegal or infringing.

Your plans

You confirm that you have the right to use the plans you send.
